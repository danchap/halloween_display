Controls

Impasse du Rossignol, Les Portes-en-Ré. Open this with C, the Controls button, or a double-tap on the view.

Orbit

Drag to turn, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Keys 1 to 6 jump to the views.

Legs

Drag an orange foot: it slides on its wall. Drag an orange knee: it moves along the alley and up and down. Hold Shift, Ctrl or Alt while dragging a knee to move it across the alley. The balls show while "Mark knees and feet" is on. Each leg moves on its own.

Walk

W A S D or the arrows move, drag or click the view to look around, Shift hurries, F flies (Space up, X down). Any move key takes over from the path.

On a phone

While walking, drag the left half of the view to move and the right half to look.
