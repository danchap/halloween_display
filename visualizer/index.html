Alley spider

Impasse du Rossignol, Les Portes-en-Ré. Orbit: drag to turn, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan, wheel to zoom, keys 1–6 for views. Walk: W A S D or arrows, drag or click the view to look around, Shift to hurry, F to fly. On a phone, drag the left half to move and the right half to look.

Design

Save as… keeps the parameters as they are now under a name, in this browser. Load replaces them with a saved design or the brief defaults.

Brief ratios

Back-pair bend, degrees off straight (front pair gets half):
Apply sets every size from L and the chosen bend, then slides each foot along its wall until the lower segment has the brief's length. Everything below stays editable afterwards.

Legs

Feet and knees are moved in the view while "Mark knees and feet" is on. Every orange ball moves in the vertical plane along the alley walls: a foot slides on its wall, a knee moves along the alley and up and down. Hold Shift, Ctrl or Alt while dragging a knee to move it across the alley instead. Each leg moves on its own. The bend buttons above re-place every knee for its current upper segment length.
